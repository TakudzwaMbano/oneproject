Nutec or Wendy house?

Both go up quickly. The right one depends on how long you want it to last and how you plan to use it.

Nutec houseWendy house
MaterialFibre-cement boards on a frameTimber
Best forPermanent homes, offices, rental unitsGarden rooms, studios, play space
Weather and pestsResists rot, termites and fireNeeds regular sealing and treatment
UpkeepRepaint every few yearsRe-varnish or repaint more often
SpeedQuick to build compared with brickFastest to put up

Questions we get asked

How long does a Nutec house take to build?

It depends on size and site conditions. Send us your plan and we'll give you a timeline with the quote.

Do I need municipal approval?

Permanent structures such as Nutec houses usually need approved plans from your municipality. Rules differ by area, so check with yours before building. We can advise on what to prepare.

What does a Nutec house cost?

Price depends on floor area, finishes and services (plumbing, electrical). Tell us what you want and we'll quote it in writing.

Can you do the plumbing and electrical as well?

Yes. We handle plumbing, electrical, flooring, painting and paving, so one team covers the whole job.

Do you deliver outside the big cities?

We serve clients across South Africa. Message us your location and we'll confirm.
